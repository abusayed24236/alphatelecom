FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions?
We've Got Answers.

Here are some common questions about our digital service assistance.

What services are available at Alpha Telecom?

We provide assistance with a range of online applications, form fill-up, document services, printing, banking-related assistance and other services that are available and supported at our centre. Please contact us to confirm whether a specific service is currently available.

Do you provide AEPS services?

AEPS-related services may be available subject to authorised provider support, system availability and applicable customer eligibility requirements. Contact us to confirm the services currently supported at our centre.

Can you help with online form fill-up?

Yes, we can assist with eligible online form-filling and application processes where the required service is available. Requirements depend on the relevant application and issuing authority.

How can I contact Alpha Telecom?

You can call us directly or send a WhatsApp message using the contact buttons on this website. You can also visit our service centre at the listed location.

What documents should I bring?

Required documents vary by service. Please contact us before visiting so that you can confirm the applicable document requirements. Never share your banking PIN, OTP or password with anyone.

Are all services available every day?

Service availability may depend on working hours, internet connectivity, government portals, authorised service providers and the rules applicable to each service. Please contact us for confirmation.
